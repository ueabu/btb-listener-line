How it behaves
Everything is for airNo "just answer it" path. Every submission is a clip you can play.
Two destinationsLast Week in Tech is the default. "An upcoming episode" opens the list of planned topics you set, like the SDLC episode.
Question, thought, or introPicking Intro shows a line they can read to open the segment, with their name filled in.
Record, audio, or videoEverything is converted to a small MP3 in the browser, capped at 2 minutes. Video only ever sends its audio.
Consent is requiredThey can't send until they tick the box.
Where it sendsOne webhook URL at the top of the file. Point it at any service that accepts a file upload (Zapier, Make, a small serverless function) and it drops into a Drive or Dropbox folder.
